What Is Pro Se Network?

Pro Se Network is a free, AI-powered legal help platform built for people who represent themselves in court. Jeremy is a step-by-step divorce engine for New York uncontested divorce, covering intake, verification, document drafting, and NYSCEF filing.

Jeremy doesn't replace a lawyer. He helps you understand your rights, prepare your filings, and navigate the system — for $0.
